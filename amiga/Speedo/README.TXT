Speedo - outline fonts for EdWord
=================================

EdWord lists the Speedo (.SPD) fonts in this drawer with the Amiga fonts in
FONTS:, and draws them itself at any size, so they look the same on screen,
in print and in PDFs. Keep this drawer next to EdWord. More Speedo fonts
(those of Atari SpeedoGDOS, for example) can go here too, or in a drawer
assigned as SPEEDO:.

These eight fonts are the ones XGDOS has. They were donated by Bitstream
Inc. to the X Consortium and shipped with X11 (font-bitstream-speedo
1.0.0), and keep their own Bitstream licence, in COPYING.TXT, which allows
them to be used, copied, modified and redistributed as long as that notice
stays with them. BITSTREAM CHARTER is a registered trademark of Bitstream
Inc.

  FONT0649.SPD   Bitstream Charter Italic
  FONT0709.SPD   Bitstream Charter Black
  FONT0710.SPD   Bitstream Charter Black Italic
  FONT0419.SPD   Courier 10 Pitch

  FONT0583.SPD   Courier 10 Pitch Bold
