spd - Speedo outline fonts for EdWord on the Amiga
==================================================

XGDOS's Speedo decoder (spd.c), scan converter (raster.c), Atari character
map (charmap.c) and fixed point helpers (xfix.c, sintab.c), copied from
XGDOS (github.com/FrankBRetro/xgdos, src/). XGDOS is under the GPL version 2;
these copies are dual-licensed by their author, Francis C Bullen: GPL 2 or,
as part of EdWord, EdWord's MIT licence (../../LICENSE).
